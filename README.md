## Include anything else you want to say, such as the difficulties you faced or overcame, the things you learned, what you enjoyed, etc, in a readme file.
Our original idea seems very ambitious compared to the final outcome. However, given the time constraint, our project turned out to be very successful. Per our proposal, we accomplished our main goal: we designed a haunted maze that includes a “monster” chasing the player. We even went a step further and decorated the interior and walls with additional designs. We had issues with downloading one software, so we adjusted and worked on Unity. It took days of creating, re-trying, and restarting until it finally started working. 

I surprisingly enjoyed designing the posters that decorated the walls. As the time went on, I found myself having fun creating the quotes and announcements that college students dread to hear! (Shalini)
